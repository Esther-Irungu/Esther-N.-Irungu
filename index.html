About

I am a cross-functional researcher who conducts demand-driven research and delivers evidence-based policy recommendations. My training spans economics, statistics and data science, and my work covers social protection, labour productivity, climate finance, digital policy and AI governance. I use statistical tools to advance inclusive and sustainable development, and I mentor young women and early-career professionals through the Global Mentorship Initiative and the Mastercard Foundation Global Give Back Circle.
